# Perry antennas and mounting

Emory Hospital Perry, 1120 Morningside Drive, Perry, GA 31069. Ground elevation 359 ft AMSL.

Use two MikroTik LHG XL 5 ac radios. The radio and the 27 dBi grid are one unit. Mount each with the V arrow up, on the pole clamps shipped in the box, on a 1.5-inch galvanized mast. There is no separate antenna and no coax.

Use a non-penetrating roof mount on the north side of the roof so the building is not in front of the dishes. Do not cut the roof membrane unless facilities accepts a penetration and a warranty repair. A stamped structural letter sets the ballast before the mount goes up.

| Dish | Faces | True bearing | Compass if declination is 6.5° W | Center above ground |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Upper | EOC | 15.6° | 22.1° | 50 ft |
| Lower | Warner Robins | 22.9° | 29.4° | 45 ft |

These bearings are 7.3° apart. Keep the Warner Robins dish at 45 ft. That hop is the tight one, with about 13 ft of bare-earth spare. If the roof structure allows, raise the EOC dish toward 55 ft so the pair has about 10 ft of vertical split. The short hop has the spare for that extra height.

Confirm declination with the NOAA calculator on the install day. Tighten the clamps at the peak AREDN SNR, with someone at the far end watching.

Bond the mount to building steel with #6 copper. Run shielded outdoor Cat6 in a drip loop, connector at the dish pointed down. Put one Ethernet surge protector per dish at the building entry, on the outdoor side of the 24 V injector, on the same ground as the mount.

Check the pinch point near 32.5509, −83.6641, about 6.9 miles toward Warner Robins, and the ridge about 2.9 miles out. Add a foot of antenna height for each foot of tree that stands in the Fresnel.
