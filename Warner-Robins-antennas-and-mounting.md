# Warner Robins antennas and mounting

Emory Hospital Warner Robins, 1601 Watson Boulevard, Warner Robins, GA 31093. Ground elevation 429 ft AMSL.

Use two MikroTik LHG XL 5 ac radios. The radio and the 27 dBi grid are one unit. Mount each with the V arrow up, on the pole clamps shipped in the box, on a 1.5-inch galvanized mast. There is no separate antenna and no coax.

This roof is the high end of both paths. Measure the roof, then make up the difference to 65 ft above ground with a non-penetrating roof mount and a short mast. Do not cut the roof membrane unless facilities accepts a penetration and a warranty repair. A stamped structural letter sets the ballast before the mount goes up.

| Dish | Faces | True bearing | Compass if declination is 6.5° W | Center above ground |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Upper | EOC | 207.4° | 213.9° | 65 ft |
| Lower | Perry | 202.9° | 209.4° | 55 ft |

These two bearings are only 4.5° apart, inside one antenna beam, so the 10 ft vertical split is required. Set the mount on the south or southwest roof corner, with parapets and penthouses behind the dishes. If the roof is already near 65 ft, keep a short pole and the same 10 ft split.

Confirm declination with the NOAA calculator on the install day. Tighten the clamps at the peak AREDN SNR, with someone at the far end watching.

Bond the mount to building steel with #6 copper. Run shielded outdoor Cat6 in a drip loop, connector at the dish pointed down. Put one Ethernet surge protector per dish at the building entry, on the outdoor side of the 24 V injector, on the same ground as the mount.

The hop back to the EOC has about 22 ft of bare-earth spare at 65 ft. The hop to Perry is the long path and the tight one. Keep the lower dish at 55 ft unless the path survey finds trees, in which case add a foot of height for each foot of intrusion.
