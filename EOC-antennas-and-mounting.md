# EOC antennas and mounting

Houston County Emergency Operations Center, 102 Chief Williams Drive, Kathleen, GA 31047. Ground elevation 369 ft AMSL.

Use two MikroTik LHG XL 5 ac radios. The radio and the 27 dBi grid are one unit. Mount each with the V arrow up, on the pole clamps shipped in the box, on a 1.5-inch galvanized mast. There is no separate antenna and no coax.

The fire and emergency management building is too low for a roof mast to reach these heights. Use one 60 ft Rohn 25G-class house-bracket tower on the building. If the structural engineer will not accept a bracket, use a guyed 60 ft tower instead.

| Dish | Faces | True bearing | Compass if declination is 6.5° W | Center above ground |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Upper | Warner Robins | 27.4° | 33.9° | 60 ft |
| Lower | Perry | 195.6° | 202.1° | 50 ft |

The two shots are 168° apart, so one mast carries both dishes. Put the Warner Robins dish at the top and the Perry dish 10 ft below it. Place the tower where both bearings clear the hose tower, flagpole, and trees on the lot.

Confirm declination with the NOAA calculator on the install day. A compass only has to land the dish inside the 7° beam. Tighten the clamps at the peak AREDN SNR, with someone at the far end watching.

Bond the tower to the building grounding electrode system with #6 copper. A licensed electrician makes that bond. Call Georgia 811 before any excavation for a rod or guy anchor. Run shielded outdoor Cat6 in a drip loop, with the connector at the dish pointed down. Put one Ethernet surge protector per dish at the building entry, on the outdoor side of the 24 V injector, on the same ground as the tower.

These heights clear bare earth with about 22 ft of spare toward Warner Robins and about 15 ft toward Perry. They do not include trees. Walk the pinch point near 32.5534, −83.6700 before ordering steel. Add a foot of antenna height for each foot of tree that stands in the Fresnel.
